# Contributing

Thanks for taking a look at this project. All contributions, bug reports, bug fixes, documentation improvements, enhancements, and ideas are welcome. It's a small script, so there's no heavy process.

## Found a bug?

Open an issue and tell me what you typed in, what you expected, and what actually happened. Mentioning your OS helps too.

## Want to fix or add something?

1. Fork the repo and clone your fork.
2. Make a new branch for your change.
3. Edit, then run `./simple-interest.sh` to check it still works.
4. Commit, push to your fork, and open a pull request.

Keep pull requests small and say what you changed and why.

## Have an idea?

Open an issue and describe it. Even half-formed ideas are fine.

## Be nice

Please follow the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
